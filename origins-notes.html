Additional Notes

There is no confirmed upper limit on Kern storage capacity. Some anecdotal reports associate unusually high Kern reserves with bloating, though this correlation is generally regarded as unreliable, given that the reports tend to originate from individuals already known for unrelated excessive water consumption.

The setting is intended as a hybrid: a shinobi-style social structure — formal ranks, missions, ceremony, clan prestige — layered over a recognizably modern world, using genuine chemistry and physics terminology alongside institutions such as diplomas and field certification rather than purely mystical training.
