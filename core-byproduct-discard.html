Core Mechanics

Byproduct and Discard

Any leftover material generated as a side effect of a reaction is called Byproduct. When that Byproduct is unstable or has no further use, it is reclassified as Discard — the system's equivalent of slag.

Experienced practitioners treat Discard as a resource rather than waste. It is common for a practitioner to carry prepared reagents or scrap material specifically as a donor stock — a pouch of processed metal is, functionally, a supply of spare atomic components carried into the field like ammunition.
