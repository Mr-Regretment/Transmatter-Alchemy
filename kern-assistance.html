Kern & The Cell

Kern Assistance

Kern cannot be handed from one practitioner to another the way a battery charge might be transferred in other magic systems — there is no direct gifting of reserves. What is possible instead is Kern Assistance: a practitioner forces their own ignited flow to follow the exact shape of another practitioner's flow, effectively submitting their output in service of someone else's ongoing process, rather than clashing against it.

This is the same underlying mechanic as an interruption clash, run in the opposite direction — cooperative instead of adversarial. Where a clash contests control of the target material, assistance lends effort toward it, reducing how much of their own Kern the recipient needs to spend to sustain the technique.

The relief scales with how much output the assisting practitioner contributes relative to the recipient's own flow. Matching their output roughly halves the Kern the recipient has to supply themselves. Contributing at several times their output reduces the recipient's own burden proportionally further — contributing at nine times their flow, for instance, leaves the recipient covering only about a tenth of the effort. This gives high-reserve practitioners a legitimate support role distinct from brute-forcing a technique themselves: propping up someone else's work rather than replacing it.
