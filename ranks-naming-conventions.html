Naming Conventions

“Transmatter: [reaction]” designates a triggered process or reaction — for example, Transmatter: Chemical Combustion. No destination material is specified, since none is being chosen; a reaction is simply being initiated. This format is typically associated with electron-level work and lower-ranked practitioners.

“Alchemy to [material]” designates a deliberate transmutation toward a specific end state — for example, Alchemy to Ferrous Oxide. This format implies the selection of an outcome rather than the triggering of a process, and is generally associated with nucleon-level work available from Tertiary rank onward.

This convention is not formally enforced within the setting, but functions as a reliable shorthand for gauging a technique's danger before its effects resolve.
