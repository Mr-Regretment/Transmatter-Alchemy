Density and Preparation

Denser materials are preferable because density correlates directly with atomic yield — more atoms occupy the same workable volume, giving a practitioner more to draw from without needing to reach for an additional material source.

Most raw materials require preparation before use — chemical treatment or physical processing that loosens the material's structure and makes its atomic components easier to extract or insert. Only advanced T-M Chemists are capable of bypassing this step entirely, working directly with untreated raw material, and even they pay a significant Kern premium for doing so.
