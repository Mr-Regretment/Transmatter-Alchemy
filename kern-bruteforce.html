Kern & The Cell

Brute Force: The Theoretical Ceiling

A practitioner with sufficiently vast Kern reserves could, in principle, dispense with technique entirely — forcing atomic or nuclear change through raw energy output rather than precision, without relying on any clean pattern or efficient chaining.

Sustaining this as a viable strategy requires reserves on the order of ten million times an average practitioner's capacity, which keeps the concept effectively mythical rather than a practical option for anyone outside legend. Notably, there is no inherent biological self-destruct limit at this scale — a practitioner with genuinely sufficient reserves can channel that output without catastrophic bodily failure as a hard rule. Brute force was never constrained by survivability. It is constrained entirely by scale, which few, if any, practitioners will ever approach.

This also implies that "Divine User" status may describe two fundamentally different archetypes: the practitioner who invents flawless techniques in real time, and the practitioner who never required finesse because their raw output made precision unnecessary. Both converge on the same theoretical rank.
