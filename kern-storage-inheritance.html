Kern & The Cell

Storage and Inheritance

Kern is stored in a biological structure referred to as the Cell. Cell capacity scales with physical health, stamina, physical strength, and repeated use of T-M itself — the body adapts to accommodate continued practice.

Cell capacity is also hereditary, and it compounds across generations. A practitioner descended from a long line of dedicated T-M users inherits a materially higher baseline capacity than one who does not — a form of accelerated, use-driven evolution. Given sufficient generational investment, this produces effective dynasties: families whose members possess significant raw capacity well before any formal training begins.
