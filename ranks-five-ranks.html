The Five Ranks

The rank structure is loosely modeled on shinobi hierarchy, chosen for how cleanly it implies both a competence tier and a social standing without requiring extensive additional explanation.

FormalInformalEquivalentCapabilities
PrimaryStarterGeninClassroom instruction, prepared textbook techniques. Electron-level only; nucleon-level work is entirely off-limits.
SecondaryUserChuninField-capable, still reliant on a booklet for reliable execution. Cleared to study dangerous techniques, not yet perform them. Basic missions begin here.
TertiaryAdvanced UserJonin (Gai/Kakashi tier)Operating from memory rather than reference material. Nucleon-level manipulation becomes accessible, with real risk.
QuaternaryCreating UserKage tierCapable of originating new technique patterns. Cleared for difficult and experimental missions when authorized.
QuinaryDivine UserJinchōriki tierTheoretical, unconfirmed. Reputed to invent and execute entirely new nucleon-level techniques in real time.
