Kern & The Cell

Failure States

Two primary failure modes exist. Overdraw occurs when a practitioner exceeds their Cell's capacity or attempts a technique beyond their rank, risking burnout, cellular damage, or the same instability and radiation exposure associated with a failed nucleon-level reaction. Interruption is the second — an active disruption of an in-progress reaction by another practitioner, covered in the next chapter.
