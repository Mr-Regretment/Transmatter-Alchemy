Overview

Transmatter Alchemy (T-M) is a magic system built on a simple premise: what if alchemy were governed by real chemistry — and at its most advanced, real nuclear physics — with an internal energy source standing in for the equipment a laboratory would normally require.

That energy is called Kern. Kern is not the effect itself; it is the force that powers a forced rearrangement of a material's electrons, protons, and neutrons. Practitioners do not conjure results from nothing. The system rests on a strict conservation principle: any atomic component added to one material must be drawn from another, and any component removed has to go somewhere. Nothing is created or destroyed outright — only moved.

The result reads less like traditional spellcasting and more like an aggressive, compressed form of applied science.

Setting

T-M is designed as a hybrid: a shinobi-style social structure — formal ranks, missions, ceremony, clan prestige — layered over a recognizably modern world, using genuine chemistry and physics terminology alongside institutions such as diplomas and field certification rather than purely mystical training.

Part of a series of fleshed-out magic systems made available for public use. Adapt this freely for stories, games, or settings of your own.
